The Climb

Four weeks a step. Every step your body changes. When you reach the top, drop back to Step 1 and climb again, with the next workout of each phase.

How the boxes fill

A week box fills when you finish 3 workouts in that week (ticked from Play or List). Half-filled means you did 1 or 2. You decide when to climb: the button lights up after week 4, but it's your call either way. Consistent for four weeks and feeling stronger? Climb.
